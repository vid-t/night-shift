## 6. Not verified

- The answer text has not been read by eye against charts. The plan asks for 100 examples to be checked before training.
- The direction words come from a simple rule: a shift of the mean larger than three times the noise in the 60 seconds before, or 2% of the tag's normal-file range. Slow ramps (`-ST`, `-LT` scenarios) may read as `stays flat` early in the window.
- Points named in the attack table that differ from the scenario code (for example 22.04 A301, A304-A306) are kept as printed unless a fix is listed above. The loop label comes from the scenario code, not the points.
- The loop label says which loop the manual says was attacked. It does not check that the attack is visible in that loop's series.
- Attack windows can overlap a second attack on another loop. Those loops are labelled `attack` too, with their own start second.
- Gradient boosting uses shape statistics only (no absolute levels), to stop it from telling files apart by their set points. It has not been tuned.
- The manual's hours per file (p. 2) were not compared with the row counts; the row counts come from the files.
